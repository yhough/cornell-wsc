About WSC

Who we are

Cornell Wall Street Club

Cornell Wall Street Club prepares students to build long-term careers across finance by developing transferable skills, providing exposure to the breadth of Wall Street, and connecting members with professionals, MBA students, and future graduate-school opportunities.

Through four specialized finance tracks, members develop skills that extend across roles and industries while exploring paths from investment banking and sales & trading to private credit, alternative assets, investment management, and emerging areas of finance.

Our goal is not simply to help members land their first internship or job. We give them the skills, network, and perspective to navigate an entire career.

Transferable Skills

Financial analysis, valuation, markets, research, modeling, communication, leadership, and commercial judgment that remain valuable across roles.

Expansive Network

Relationships with industry professionals, recruiters, Cornell alumni, MBA students, and a community of ambitious peers.

Career Flexibility

Preparation for careers that evolve—from banking to private credit, research to asset management, or finance to graduate business education.

Industry Access

Workshops, panels, firm visits, and conversations that reveal opportunities across the broader financial ecosystem.

Explore the Financial Ecosystem

You do not need to know exactly where you want to end up. WSC introduces members to opportunities beyond the handful of roles most students encounter first.

  • Private Credit, Secondaries & Alternative Assets
  • Infrastructure, Real Assets & Restructuring
  • Capital Markets, Research & Institutional Sales
  • Investor Relations, Strategic Finance & Family Offices

MBA & Graduate Business Exposure

Members learn how graduate business education can fit into a long-term Wall Street career strategy through conversations, resources, and programming.

  • Cornell MBA students and MBA alumni
  • Deferred MBA students and admits
  • Admissions and program representatives, where available
  • Application strategy, testing, resumes, essays, and career stories

Explore Deferred MBA Opportunities

Members can learn about leading deferred-enrollment pathways and prepare for the application process through information sessions, GMAT/GRE planning, resume development, essay preparation, and conversations with current MBA students and deferred admits.

  • Wharton — Moelis Advance Access Program
  • Harvard Business School — 2+2
  • Columbia Business School — Deferred Enrollment Program
  • UVA Darden — Future Year Scholars Program
  • Cornell Johnson — MBA and graduate business pathways

Mentorship Programs

Two signature programs designed to take members from fundamentals to offer.

Wall Street Mentorship

A rigorous, end-to-end foundation in finance fundamentals, valuation, and core deal processes — translating theory into practical, interview-ready skill sets through weekly workshops, case studies, and training led by experienced mentors and alumni.

  • Core Financial & Valuation Concepts
  • Financial Modeling Essentials
  • Industry Applications
  • Accounting & Financial Statement Analysis
  • Leveraged Buyouts (LBOs)
  • Advanced Deal Analysis

Interview Preparation Mentorship

Develops the communication, problem-solving, and analytical skills essential for finance recruiting. Members receive one-on-one feedback, mock interview sessions, and exposure to real formats used by leading firms.

  • Professional Development & Recruiting Strategy
  • Behavioral & Fit Interview Training
  • Projects, Practice & Peer Development
